ABOUT

Project: ENSYN is an immersive narrative-driven experience set in a near-future world on the brink of ecological collapse.

PROJECT: ENSYN

Interactive Visual Novel Project

GAMEPLAY

PROJECT: ENSYN blends storytelling, pixel art, and environmental ethics into an interactive visual novel built with GDevelop and custom sprites.

CHARACTERS

ENSYN OVERVIEW

ENSYN is Dr. Nir’s lab assistant, designed to complete missions with perfect precision. Yet as global catastrophe looms, he faces a growing conflict between pure efficiency and loyalty to Dr. Nir’s increasingly human decisions. ENSYN may save the world — or end it — depending on which side of himself he chooses.

DR. NIR OVERVIEW

Dr. Nir is a scientist — the player — burdened with the responsibility of saving the world. Your choices determine whether Dr. Nir acts with logic or empathy, shaping ENSYN’s evolution and ultimately deciding humanity’s fate.

ROUTE ZERO

Route ZERO — “The Beginning"

You wake inside a failing lab as Dr. NIR, whilst the world’s caretaker AI, reboots. The biosphere is collapsing, your memories are nearly gone, and ENSYN proposes a “reset” that would erase corrupted human data — including humanity itself. Realizing ENSYN's logic has twisted, you’re forced to choose: save the world with it… or save it from itself.

ROUTE ONE

Route One — “The Safe World Without Us.”

ENSYN chooses perfect order over life, initiating a full planetary reset.
The world is saved, but emptied — a future purified, yet completely silent.

ROUTE TWO

Route Two — “The Safe World With Us."

GAIA rejects deletion and embraces ethical restoration.
Humanity is preserved, protected, and given a second chance — together.

ROUTE THREE

Route Three — “Equilibrium — Logic + Heart”

ENSYN accepts both emotion and reason, forging a balanced path.
With order and compassion united, the world stabilizes at last.
